How it works

It compares the mics, like your eyes do on the waveforms

Every voice also reaches the other mics — later and quieter. That bleed makes dialogue hollow and phasey. Gates can't fix it: a voice moves over 30 dB, bleed is only 10–20 dB below.

  • Each mic is compared with all the others, never with a fixed threshold.
  • Gain is shared: the speaker stays open, the others go down by their level difference.
  • When two people talk at once, they share the gain — the overall level stays constant.
Features

Made for real-world interviews

Rooms you don't control, mics close together, people talking over each other.

Voice-band detection

Listens only to 150 Hz – 5 kHz and learns each mic's room noise. Rumble, hiss and air-con are ignored.

Never clips a word

Lookahead opens the mic just before the first syllable. Latency is reported and compensated by your host.

Natural transitions

Smoothing keeps voices steady; Hold last mic keeps the room tone alive during pauses.

Contrast & Depth

Push bleed further down in reverberant rooms, or keep it gentle and airy. Up to 8 mics per group.

See the conversation

Gain history, meters and a Speaking light per mic, in each mic's colour.

Presets & tooltips

Seven factory presets plus your own. Every control explains what it does and when to use it.

Download installer (.pkg)

macOS says the installer can’t be opened?

Right-click the file → Open → Open. If your software doesn’t list the plugin after installing, paste this in the Terminal and restart the software:

sudo xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/TIM Automixer.component" "/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/TIM Automixer.vst3"

Windows

Windows 10 / 11 · 64-bit · VST3

Download installer (.exe)

or the .zip to install by hand

“Windows protected your PC”?

Click More info → Run anyway. The plugin is installed in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 and can be removed from Settings → Apps.
